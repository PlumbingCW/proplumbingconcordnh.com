Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Concord, NH.

A garbage disposal that leaks under the sink or backs up the kitchen drain can ruin a cabinet fast, so our line is open 24/7 for disposal calls in Concord. Turn the unit off at the switch, unplug it or flip the breaker and never put your hand inside the chamber.

1.

Leaking or backing up: what to do first.

Water under the sink needs a quick trace. A leak at the top flange, the dishwasher hose, the side drain or the bottom of the unit are different problems. A leak from the body or the bottom seam means the disposal has failed and needs replacing.

If the sink fills with gray water and food when the unit runs, the disposal is clogged or the drain line downstream is blocked. Stop using the sink and call us. If a dishwasher drains through the disposal, hold off running it.

2.

A humming or jammed disposal.

A hum with no grinding means the flywheel is stuck. With the power off, use an Allen wrench in the hole on the underside to turn it back and forth, then press the red reset button. That frees many jams. If it still hums or trips the breaker, the motor or bearings may be failing.

Never use your hand and never run water with chemical cleaner in a stuck unit. Fibrous foods like celery, onion skins and potato peels are classic causes, along with bones and fruit pits.

4.

Installing a new disposal.

Installation includes the mounting assembly, a new drain connection, the dishwasher inlet and the electrical connection. Many disposals are plugged into an outlet under the sink, but some are hard-wired, and we check this before we install.

Horsepower matters. A half-horsepower unit handles light use. A three-quarter or one horsepower model suits a busy family. Septic homes in nearby towns need extra care with grinding, since disposals add solids to the tank. See our Loudon page for how septic changes the advice.

5.

How to keep one running.

Habits that help.

  • 1.Run cold water before, during and after use.
  • 2.Feed scraps gradually, not in a pile.
  • 3.Avoid grease, coffee grounds in bulk, bones and fibrous peels.
  • 4.Grind a handful of ice now and then to knock debris off.
  • 5.Fix a slow kitchen drain before it overloads the disposal.

A disposal that keeps jamming or a kitchen drain that stays slow may need more than a repair. The line beyond it can be coated with grease, and the fix is on our hydro jetting page.

6.

What a replacement visit includes.

  1. Power off and disconnect. We cut power at the switch or breaker and disconnect the drain and dishwasher hose.
  2. Remove the old unit. The mounting ring comes off and the disposal comes down.
  3. Mount the new one. A fresh gasket, mounting ring and drain connection go in, and the dishwasher inlet is opened if needed.
  4. Wire or plug in. We connect the power and confirm the unit is protected.
  5. Test. We run water and grind, check every joint for leaks and listen for vibration.

Price depends on the unit you choose, whether the electrical setup needs changes and how the drain under the sink is arranged. You hear the number before we start.
