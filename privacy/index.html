Privacy Policy.

Effective November 14, 2026. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site or call us.

Information we collect

This website does not have forms, so it does not collect information you type. When you call (603) 815-9179, we collect your phone number, your address and the details of your plumbing problem so we can dispatch a plumber.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call after hearing that notice, you agree to the recording.

How we use it

We use call information to dispatch, schedule and perform plumbing work, to follow up on service and to improve quality. We do not sell your information.

Embedded maps and third parties

Our pages include maps provided by Google. Google may collect information such as your IP address when the map loads, under its own privacy policy. We do not control that collection.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to operate and secure the site.

Sharing

We share information with the technicians and service providers who help us complete your job, and when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by calling or by emailing contact@proplumbingconcordnh.com. We will respond as the law requires.
