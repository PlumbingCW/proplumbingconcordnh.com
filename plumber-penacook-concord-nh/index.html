Plumber in Penacook, Concord, NH.

Penacook is the old mill village at the northern edge of Concord, and part of it lies in Boscawen. If you have a plumbing problem here, call and give us your street. We will ask which town you are in, because that decides who handles permits and who owns the sewer main outside your house. For the rest of the city, see our Concord, NH plumbing page.

1.

Where Penacook sits.

The City of Concord's Master Plan 2030 describes Penacook as a former mill village on the northern edge of the city, partly in Boscawen, with its central business district on Village Street. The plan names Elm, Washington, Bonney, Merrimack, Summer, High, Shaw and Coral among its historic residential streets.

Penacook does not have an official boundary line, so we go by your street address rather than by a map outline. If you are unsure whether your address counts as Penacook, that is fine. Give us the street and the town on your mail.

2.

What a mill village's housing means for a plumbing call.

According to the Master Plan, Penacook has larger houses built for mill owners and worker housing in the form of side-hall houses and duplexes. Newer homes sit alongside them. We do not assume what is inside any wall from the age of a street. A camera, a pressure test and a look at the water heater tell us more than a date does.

In a duplex, ask before you call whether the water heater, main shutoff and drain serve one side or both. Shared walls and shared plumbing are how a leak on one side can show up on the other. The leak detection page explains how we trace a leak without opening walls first.

3.

Sewer service in Penacook.

The City of Concord says the Penacook Wastewater Treatment Plant serves the Penacook area of Concord and part of Boscawen. The City maintains the sewer lateral inside the public right-of-way. The property owner is responsible from that point to the building.

The City lists roots, grease, sagging, fatigue, foreign objects, freezing and damage as the usual causes of lateral trouble. If a drain backs up more than once, a sewer camera inspection shows which of those it is, and sewer line cleaning clears it. A backup that affects the whole street is a call to the City's General Services at 603-228-2737.

4.

Winter in the north end of the city.

Concord's frost depth is 48 inches and the January average low is about 13 degrees Fahrenheit, according to the City. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated porches and crawlspaces are the ones that split. If a line freezes, shut the main valve before it thaws and call. Burst pipe repair covers what to do in the first few minutes.

5.

Before you call from Penacook.

  • Confirm your town. Concord or Boscawen, from your tax bill or mail.
  • Find the main shutoff. And note whether a second unit uses the same one.
  • Note where the water heater is. Basement, closet or attic.
  • Describe the drain. One fixture, several fixtures or the whole house.
  • Keep the phone on. We may call back with questions.
