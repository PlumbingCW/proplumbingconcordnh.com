Emergency Water Heater Repair in Concord, NH.

No hot water on a cold Concord morning is an urgent problem, and our line is open 24/7 for water heater repair. Before we arrive, check that the breaker has not tripped or the gas valve is on, and if the tank is leaking, shut off the cold supply and the power or gas.

1.

No hot water: the first checks.

Start with the simple causes. On an electric unit, look at the breaker and the red reset button on the upper thermostat. On a gas unit, make sure the gas valve is on and the pilot is lit. A tripped breaker that resets and holds is a minor event. One that trips again is a failing element or a short.

If the water is lukewarm instead of cold, a single element in an electric tank may have failed, or the dip tube has broken and is mixing cold water into the top of the tank. Both are common repairs. Call us and we will tell you how to narrow it down.

3.

A leaking water heater.

Not every leak means a dead tank. Drips can come from the relief valve, a loose inlet or outlet fitting, a drain valve that is not fully closed or even condensation on a cold unit. We trace the source before telling you anything, because the repair for a fitting is a short visit and a failed tank is a replacement.

If water comes from the tank body or the seam at the base, turn off the supply and call. We can arrange a same-day replacement when stock allows. Leaving a leaking tank running will damage floors, drywall and stored items quickly.

A relief valve that discharges often points to thermal expansion. When a house has a check valve or pressure-reducing valve on the incoming line, heated water has nowhere to go. The fix is usually an expansion tank, and fitting one is a repair we do on the spot.

4.

Gas, electric and hybrid units.

Gas heaters add venting and combustion to the list. A flame that burns yellow, soot around the draft hood or a burning smell calls for a shutoff, not a fix. If you smell gas, leave, call 911 or your gas utility, and only then call us. We will not restart a gas heater that shows signs of back-drafting until the venting is sound.

Electric tanks are simpler. Elements and thermostats are inexpensive and easy to test with a meter. Hybrid heat pump units add a compressor and a condensate drain, and most of their service calls come from clogged condensate lines or blocked air filters.

5.

Tankless units need their own care.

A tankless heater that shuts off mid-shower, shows an error code or runs lukewarm is usually scaled up or starved of gas. Flushing with a descaling solution fixes many of them. In soft Concord water the scale builds slowly, but private wells nearby can be a different story. Our tankless water heater page covers flushing in detail.

6.

When repair stops making sense.

Repairs on a tank over about 12 years old are a judgment call. A new element on a tank with a rusted shell is money spent on a short-lived fix. We tell you honestly when the repair will hold and when the better use of your money is a new unit.

The same goes for a unit that has needed two repairs in two years. We give you both prices so the comparison is concrete, and the choice stays with you.
